# Stage C: brain visualization

## What was inspected

The audit's description of the asset still holds: FreeSurfer `fsaverage6` pial surface, 81,924 vertices, 163,840 triangles, atlas lobe weights and sulcal depth baked per vertex. After Stage B the surface was a single `MeshPhysicalMaterial` (roughness 0.74, sheen, no clearcoat) under three directional lights and a small environment map. That is why it read as carved stone: an opaque, evenly lit, single colour solid.

## Approaches considered

| | Approach | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A | Translucent anatomical shell only | Fixes the heaviness, but a still, evenly lit glass object has no sense of activity. |
| B | Neural illumination only (lines and glow over the old surface) | Light effects on an opaque matte solid look applied rather than intrinsic, and they need a dark page to show. |
| C | Hybrid: translucent shell with restrained surface activity | **Chosen.** The shell carries the anatomy and the material quality. The activity layer adds life without carrying the image. |

C suits this asset because the mesh already holds the two signals the look needs, sulcal depth and lobe membership, so the whole treatment is driven by vertex data with no textures.

## What was built

All in `components/brain-experience/brain-materials.ts`, driven by `brain-model.tsx`.

**Layer A, cortical shell.** A custom shader in place of the physical material.
- Light is computed in the shader: a wrapped key and a fill, in view space, so no side falls to black.
- Folds come from the anatomical sulcal depth channel, coloured ice blue rather than dark.
- A view dependent edge makes the silhouette solid and luminous and the facing surface airy.
- Translucency uses two passes: depth only, then colour. The colour pass blends with the page but never with the far side of the brain, so there are no sorting artifacts on the folded surface.
- Two presets, blended by page tone: pearl and ice blue with an azure edge on paper, a blue body with a cyan white edge on night.

**Layer B, neural activity.** 60 thin ribbons lying on the cortex, in one draw call.
- Paths are generated in `scripts/build-brain.mjs` by walking mesh edges along gyral crowns, so they follow real folds.
- Each carries one slow pulse of light per 6 to 11 seconds, then rests.
- **They are decorative.** They are not tractography and do not represent measured connections. The data file says so in its first field.

**Layer C, lobe emphasis.** Replaces the full orange fill.
- The highlighted lobe is lifted with light and a cool tint. Folds stay visible inside it.
- The rest of the cortex recedes instead of turning navy.
- A fine outline follows the atlas boundary. Lobe weights are smoothed over neighbouring vertices at build time so the line is a clean contour, not a sawtooth along triangle edges.
- Ribbons inside the lobe brighten and the others fade.
- No orange is used on the brain.

**Finishing.** A soft halo behind the brain stands in for bloom with no post processing, and a faint ground shadow on paper sets the object in space. Scene lights and the environment map were removed, since the shaders light themselves.

## Cost

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Draw calls | 1 | 4 (depth, shell, ribbons, backdrop) |
| Download | 1.16 MB | 1.18 MB model plus 94 KB paths, loaded after first paint |
| Lighting | 4 lights, PMREM environment | none, computed in shader |
| Post processing | none | none |

The fragment shader is simpler than the physical material it replaces. The second pass over 164k triangles is the main added cost.

## Cerebellum and brainstem

Not added. Findings:

- **Same template, derived:** the right source would be FreeSurfer's own `aseg` segmentation of fsaverage, meshed into cerebellum and brainstem surfaces. It shares the coordinate space, scale, and licence, so alignment would be exact. TemplateFlow publishes only the T1 image and a brain mask for this template, not the segmentation, so it needs the file from a FreeSurfer installation. Surfaces from a 1 mm label volume would be smooth forms without fine cerebellar folia.
- **BodyParts3D (DBCLS):** real anatomical meshes, licensed CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Commercial use is allowed with attribution, but share alike applies to derivatives, and the meshes come from a different individual, so fitting them to this cortex would be by eye. Needs a licence decision.
- **Marketplace models:** usually artist made, with mixed licences and no anatomical provenance.

Recommendation: the aseg route, once someone can supply `fsaverage/mri/aseg.mgz`. Until then lateral and posterior views show cortex only, and the page states this.
